Machine learning is the umbrella term for methods where a [training algorithm](/en/glossary/training-algorithm) sets a [model](/en/glossary/model)'s [parameters](/en/glossary/parameters) from examples — instead of a human writing the rules a decision is based on by hand, in code.

The name is a little misleading: a model doesn't "learn" in the human sense — it goes through a mathematical optimization process in which its parameters get adjusted, step by step, to fit the training examples better and better. Exactly how that process works is the topic of a later, dedicated part of the site.

Explained in more depth in ["Program, Algorithm, Model — What's the Difference?"](/en/lessons/program-algorithm-model).
