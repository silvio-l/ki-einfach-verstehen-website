---
title: 'Scalar'
description: 'A single number, the simplest block of numbers, with no axis.'
translationKey: skalar
---

A scalar is a single number, such as today's noon temperature: 18 degrees. It has no **axis**, because it does not extend in any direction; its shape is empty.

In machine learning libraries a scalar counts as a [tensor](/en/glossary/tensor) with no axis. Several scalars in a fixed order form a [vector](/en/glossary/vector).
