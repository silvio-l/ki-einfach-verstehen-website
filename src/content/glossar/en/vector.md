---
title: 'Vector'
description: 'An ordered list of numbers through which a model processes properties as one numerical representation.'
translationKey: vektor
---

A vector is an ordered list of numbers. In a language model, a [token ID](/en/glossary/token-id) can serve as the address at which a learned vector is looked up.

Its components matter together: they form a numerical representation with which the model continues calculating. Several vectors can be combined into a [tensor](/en/glossary/tensor).

The transition from IDs to vectors begins in [How Language Becomes Numbers: Tokenizers, IDs, and Vocabulary](/en/lessons/tokenizer-ids-vocabulary).
