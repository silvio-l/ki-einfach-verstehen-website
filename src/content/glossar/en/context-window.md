---
title: 'Context Window'
description: 'The limited number of token positions a model can consider in one processing operation.'
translationKey: kontextfenster
---

The context window describes how many [tokens](/en/glossary/token) a model can consider during one operation. Depending on the system, the input and generated output share this limited space.

Text containing many small tokens occupies more positions than a compactly tokenized phrase of similar visible length. Word and character counts therefore do not determine context use exactly.

Explained in more depth in [How Language Becomes Numbers: Tokenizers, IDs, and Vocabulary](/en/lessons/tokenizer-ids-vocabulary).
